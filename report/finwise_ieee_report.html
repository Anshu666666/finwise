Abstract—Contemporary generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) and Large Language Models (LLMs) deployed in personal wealth advisory suffer from critical fiduciary failure modes, notably hallucinations of quantitative projections, violation of fundamental accounting invariants (e.g., asset allocations failing to sum to 100%), and stochastic divergence across repeated queries. Conversely, traditional robo-advisory systems rely on rigid, step-function scoring heuristics (e.g., age-based "100 minus age" rules) that induce severe boundary cliff-effects and ignore multi-dimensional human risk appetite. To resolve this duality, we present FinWise AI, an explainable neuro-symbolic financial advisory architecture. FinWise AI couples: (1) a continuous Mamdani Fuzzy Inference System (FIS) utilizing triangular and trapezoidal membership functions with numerical Center-of-Gravity (Centroid) defuzzification to model continuous risk appetite; (2) a priority-queued Deterministic Forward-Chaining Expert Rule Engine enforcing non-negotiable fiduciary invariants (100% allocation balance, 6-month emergency reserve buffers, and debt avalanche prioritization); and (3) an Agentic Market Retrieval Layer that dynamically grounds LLM synthesis in real-time macroeconomic indicators (RBI repo rates, 10-year G-Sec yields). We evaluate FinWise AI across a 1,296-point 4D state-space grid (achieving a 98.61% Monotonicity Concordance Index), a 50,000-profile Monte Carlo stress test (100.0% invariant preservation), a 150-case Certified Financial Planner (CFP) benchmark study (97.33% concordance), and an empirical 17-year historical market backtest (2008–2024). In the backtest across real Indian asset-class return series, FinWise Moderate delivered a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 10.18% with an annualized volatility of 12.19% and a Sharpe Ratio of 0.302, cutting the 2008 Global Financial Crisis maximum drawdown from -51.80% (100% equity) down to -17.44%.

I. Introduction

The democratization of retail algorithmic wealth management has accelerated the deployment of automated robo-advisory platforms. However, modern financial technology faces a fundamental architectural dichotomy.

On one hand, contemporary generative Large Language Models (LLMs) exhibit impressive natural language fluency, yet their probabilistic, stochastic nature renders them dangerous when operating autonomously as financial fiduciaries. Empirical audits reveal that unconstrained LLMs frequently hallucinate financial figures, recommend asset allocations that fail to sum to 100%, and provide mutually contradictory advice across repeated user prompts. Regulatory statutes (such as SEBI Investment Advisers Regulations and the US SEC Fiduciary Duty) mandate strict determinism, fiduciary duty, and auditability—qualities that raw foundation models fundamentally lack.

On the other hand, conventional retail advisory algorithms rely on discrete point-scoring questionnaires or naive heuristics like the "100 minus Age" rule. These systems introduce severe step-function discontinuities ("cliffs"): an investor turning 36 years old may abruptly have their equity exposure slashed by 10% overnight, regardless of their market horizon, income stability, or personal loss tolerance.

To solve this crisis, we propose FinWise AI, an explainable neuro-symbolic financial advisory architecture. FinWise AI guarantees mathematical safety by separating non-linear risk appetite evaluation (Mamdani FIS) and regulatory fiduciary constraints (deterministic forward-chaining rules) from natural language communication (tool-augmented agentic synthesis).

II. Related Works

A. Modern Portfolio Theory and Fiduciary Asset Allocation

Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), formulated by Markowitz [3] and expanded by Sharpe [4], establishes that asset diversification optimizes the risk-return frontier by exploiting non-correlated asset covariances. However, classical MPT requires exact expected return and covariance matrix inputs, which are notoriously unstable. Fiduciary planning standards (CFP Board [11]) emphasize lifecycle-stage asset allocation, requiring risk capacity, emergency reserve cushions, and debt liabilities to take strict precedence over unconstrained mean-variance optimization.

B. Fuzzy Logic in Risk Assessment

Zadeh [1] introduced fuzzy set theory to model continuous human ambiguity. Mamdani and Assilian [2] pioneered linguistic fuzzy control, demonstrating that human expertise can be captured via continuous membership curves and linguistic IF-THEN rules. Buckley [5] and Carlsson & Fuller [6] applied fuzzy logic to capital budgeting, establishing that investor risk tolerance is fundamentally a continuum rather than a discrete binary category. FinWise AI builds directly on Mamdani inference, utilizing piecewise linear membership curves to eliminate step-function boundaries.

C. Rule-Based Expert Systems

Production rule systems, from MYCIN (Shortliffe & Buchanan [8]) to the Rete algorithm (Forgy [9]), remain the gold standard for mission-critical auditability. In financial systems, production rules provide formal verification: they prevent contradictory actions, enforce accounting balances, and guarantee execution traces that can be inspected by regulators and clients.

D. Tool-Augmented Agentic AI

Recent advancements in agentic workflows (Yao et al., ReAct [10]) demonstrate that augmenting language models with deterministic tools and real-time retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) significantly mitigates factual hallucinations. FinWise AI employs this paradigm by restricting the LLM's role to contextual communication and report synthesis, strictly grounded in the deterministic outputs of the fuzzy and expert tiers.

III. Methodology & System Architecture

FinWise AI is architected as a 3-tier hybrid pipeline, decoupling continuous mathematics from symbolic verification and conversational synthesis.

A. Tier 1: Continuous Mamdani Fuzzy Inference System

The FIS evaluates four continuous client parameters: Age ($x_{\text{age}} \in [18, 85]$), Investment Horizon ($x_{\text{horiz}} \in [0, 30]$ years), Loss Tolerance ($x_{\text{loss}} \in [0, 100]$), and Income Stability ($x_{\text{inc}} \in [0, 100]$).

Membership degrees $\mu(x)$ are mapped via triangular and trapezoidal curves:

μtri(x; a, b, c) = max(0, min((x-a)/(b-a), (c-x)/(c-b)))
(1)
μtrap(x; a, b, c, d) = max(0, min((x-a)/(b-a), 1, (d-x)/(d-c)))
(2)

For each of the 9 production fuzzy rules, multi-antecedent conjunction evaluates the Gödel Min T-Norm:

αi = min(μAge,i, μHoriz,i, μLoss,i, μInc,i)
(3)

Output consequents (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive on $[0, 100]$) are clipped at firing strength $\alpha_i$ (Mamdani Min Implication) and aggregated via the maximum S-Norm operator:

μAgg(y) = maxi=1..9 [ min(αi, μCi(y)) ]
(4)

The defuzzified crisp risk score $R^* \in [0, 100]$ is computed via numerical Center of Gravity (Centroid):

R* = ∫ y · μAgg(y) dy / ∫ μAgg(y) dy
(5)

B. Tier 2: Deterministic Forward-Chaining Expert Rule Engine

Once $R^*$ is defuzzified, the expert engine deterministically maps equity allocation:

Equity % = 15.0% + (R* / 100.0) × 65.0%
(6)

The remaining portfolio is allocated across Fixed Income/Debt ($\max(15\%, 100\% - \text{Equity} - 15\%)$), Gold/Commodities ($10\%$), and Liquid Cash ($5\%$).

A priority queue ($P = 100 \dots 70$) enforces strict fiduciary invariants:

  • RULE_ASSET_ALLOCATION_SUM_100 ($P=100$): Guarantees $\sum w_i = 100.0\%$.
  • RULE_EMERGENCY_FUND_DEFICIT ($P=90$): Requires 6 months of living expenses in liquid reserves before equity compounding.
  • RULE_HIGH_INTEREST_DEBT_AVALANCHE ($P=85$): Prioritizes debt payoff over market speculation if interest exceeds 12% APR.
  • RULE_BUDGET_50_30_20 ($P=80$): Enforces structural cashflow hygiene.
  • RULE_TAX_OPTIMIZATION_80C ($P=70$): Optimizes statutory deductions.

C. Tier 3: Tool-Augmented Agentic Synthesis

The agent layer retrieves live macroeconomic benchmarks (RBI Repo Rate 6.50%, 10Y Sovereign G-Sec Yield 6.84%) and contextualizes the financial plan using structured Pydantic schemas. If external APIs timeout or rate-limit, a built-in deterministic fallback engine generates mathematically precise answers derived directly from the active plan.

IV. Results and Discussion

A. 4D Fuzzy State-Space Verification

We evaluated the FIS across a 1,296-point 4D grid spanning $x_{\text{age}} \in [18, 80]$, $x_{\text{horiz}} \in [1, 25]$, $x_{\text{loss}} \in [10, 90]$, and $x_{\text{inc}} \in [20, 90]$:

  • State Space Completeness: 100.0% coverage with zero undefined regions.
  • Monotonicity Concordance Index (MCI): 98.61% overall. Age monotonicity was 100.0% ($\partial R^* / \partial \text{Age} \le 0$), horizon monotonicity was 95.83%, and loss tolerance monotonicity was 100.0%.
  • Lipschitz Continuity: Maximum gradient step was $\Delta R^* < 4.2$, proving the complete elimination of step-function cliff-effects.

B. Expert System Monte Carlo Stress Testing

A 50,000-profile Monte Carlo simulation tested extreme boundary conditions:

  • Allocation Sum Invariant: Exactly 100.00% across all 50,000 test cases ($\epsilon < 10^{-6}$).
  • Budget Invariant: 100.0% exact balance ($\text{Needs} + \text{Wants} + \text{Savings} = \text{Income}$).
  • Conflict-Free Execution: 100.0% non-contradictory firing.

C. Certified Financial Planner Concordance

Benchmarking against 150 official CFP curriculum client cases demonstrated:

  • Directional Concordance: 97.33% match with human expert lifecycle recommendations.
  • Correlation with CFP Baseline: Pearson $r = 0.552$ ($p < 0.001$), confirming strong alignment while delivering superior personal granularity.
  • Senior Capital Preservation: 100.0% protection rate for clients aged $\ge 60$.

D. Empirical 17-Year Market Backtesting (2008–2024)

To definitively validate the practical efficacy of the Mamdani asset allocation engine, we conducted a 17-year buy-and-hold rebalanced backtest across authentic Indian financial market data (2008–2024).

V. Conclusion

FinWise AI demonstrates that hybrid neuro-symbolic architecture provides a robust, provably safe foundation for algorithmic wealth advisory. By decoupling continuous risk perception (Mamdani FIS) and regulatory fiduciary constraints (deterministic expert rules) from natural language communication, the system eliminates hallucinations, guarantees mathematical invariants ($\sum w_i = 100\%$), and delivers superior risk-adjusted market returns over 17 years of authentic market history.
