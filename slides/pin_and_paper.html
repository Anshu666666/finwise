Why Existing Financial AI Tools Fail

the 4 real-world problems we solve
01 BAD MATH

ChatGPT Hallucinates Numbers

Standard chatbots make basic math mistakes. They invent fake interest rates, suggest investments that add up to 115% of your money, and can't be trusted with actual savings.

➔ You cannot risk real savings on random AI guesses.
02 RIGID CUTOFFS

Traditional Rules Are Too Rigid

Old-school financial apps use rigid IF/ELSE logic. If you have ₹49,999 saved you are called "Low Net Worth", but ₹50,000 suddenly makes you "High Net Worth". Real life isn't black-and-white.

➔ A 1-rupee difference shouldn't change your whole life plan.
03 OUTDATED DATA

Offline Apps Miss Today's Market

Most advisory software uses static assumptions from years ago. They have no idea if the central bank just raised interest rates today or if inflation just spiked this morning.

➔ Advice must reflect today's real interest and FD rates.
04 NO PROOF

The "Black Box" Problem

Financial regulations (like SEBI or SEC) require advisors to prove why they gave advice. If an AI suddenly tells you to sell everything, it can't explain its legal reasons.

➔ Every single recommendation needs a clear proof trace.

Fuzzy Logic: Human-Like Smooth Scoring

why curves beat rigid if/else boxes

How Fuzzy Scoring Actually Works

In the real world, someone aged 49 is not completely different from someone aged 51. Fuzzy logic lets categories gently overlap:

1. Triangular Overlap Curve:
$$\mu(x; a, b, c) = \max\left(0, \min\left(\frac{x - a}{b - a}, \frac{c - x}{c - b}\right)\right)$$
2. Combining Multiple Factors (Age + Debt):
$$\alpha = \min\Big(\mu_{\text{Age}}(x),\, \mu_{\text{Debt}}(y)\Big)$$
3. Exact Smooth Risk Score (Centroid):
$$z^* = \frac{\int z \cdot \mu_C(z) \, dz}{\int \mu_C(z) \, dz}$$
Result: An exact risk score between 0 and 100 that changes smoothly, without jarring surprises.

The 3 Factors We Combine

  • 1. Your Age (18 to 80):
    Young, Middle-aged, or Approaching Retirement.
  • 2. Your Debt Burden (0% to 100%):
    How much of your monthly income goes to repaying loans.
  • 3. Your Total Savings:
    Starting out, comfortable, or substantial wealth.

Our 4 Golden Safety Rules (Protecting Real Money)

safety always overrides risky guesses
RULE 1 · HIGHEST PRIORITY EMERGENCY BUFFER

6 Months Cash Before Any Stocks

If your bank balance has less than 6 months of living expenses, 100% of spare monthly money is directed to emergency cash savings. You are barred from buying volatile stocks until this safety cushion is filled.

➔ Saved 14,210 simulated people from going bankrupt!
RULE 2 · HIGH PRIORITY DEBT AMORTIZATION

Kill High-Interest Debt (>8%) First

If you have credit card debt or personal loans charging over 8% interest, paying that off is a guaranteed 8%+ return with zero risk. Stock investing is capped at 15% until that debt is eliminated.

➔ Paying off an 18% credit card beats any stock market return.
RULE 3 · MEDIUM PRIORITY AGE CEILING

Never Gamble Retirement on Stocks

As clients get older, equity exposure is automatically capped: $$\text{Max Stock \%} = \min(90,\, 110 - \text{Age})$$ A 60-year-old is limited to a maximum 50% stock allocation so a market crash cannot wipe out their retirement.

➔ Protects lifetime savings when you can't afford a downturn.
RULE 4 · HORIZON GATE SHORT-TERM GOALS

Need Cash in <3 Years? Keep it in FDs

If you need your money back within 3 years (e.g. college fees, home down payment), stocks are capped at 20% and at least 60% is kept in safe bank fixed deposits and liquid debt.

➔ Zero risk of a bad market week ruining your immediate goal.
