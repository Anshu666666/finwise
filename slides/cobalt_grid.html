The Four Structural Dilemmas of Automated Wealth Advisory

DIAGNOSTIC LEDGER // 002
01 / STOCHASTIC VOLATILITY

LLM Hallucination & Arithmetic Collapse

Pure generative models cannot perform reliable quantitative portfolio optimization. Unbounded probabilistic generators hallucinate ticker yields, violate foundational capital conservation rules, and produce asset allocation weights that do not sum to 100%.

CRITICAL FAILURE: Non-deterministic weight synthesis, zero regulatory accountability, prompt-injection vulnerability.
02 / DISCRETE FRAGILITY

Boundary Cliff Effects in Decision Trees

Traditional static rule engines depend on rigid crisp thresholds. An investor with a ₹49,999 emergency fund is classified as "Vulnerable" while ₹50,001 abruptly triggers "Aggressive Equity"—an artificial discontinuous cliff that misallocates real capital.

CRITICAL FAILURE: Step-function portfolio jumps, zero tolerance for subjective financial ambiguity.
03 / REAL-TIME BLINDNESS

Stale Macroeconomic Context

Offline advisory logic operates in an economic vacuum. Hardcoded formulas cannot adapt when central banks unexpectedly raise repo rates by 50 bps, inflation spikes beyond target bands, or geopolitical conflicts drive emergency sovereign yields higher.

CRITICAL FAILURE: Outdated yield assumptions, static debt payoff guidance during interest rate shifts.
04 / COMPLIANCE PROVENANCE

The Black-Box Auditability Impasse

Financial regulations (SEBI, FINRA, SEC) mandate comprehensive explainability and formal mathematical justification for every fiduciary recommendation. End-to-end deep neural models cannot provide an audit trail of why a client was allocated 30% debt.

CRITICAL FAILURE: Regulatory rejection, impossible forensic reconstruction of investment logic.
